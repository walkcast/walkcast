# Log a new ticket

Audience: Support agents · Length: about 40 seconds

1. Every request starts as a ticket. Here's how to log one.
2. Click New ticket at the top of any page.
3. Give it a short title that says what's wrong.
4. Then set how urgent it is.
5. Choose High if people can't work until it's fixed.
6. Click Create ticket. It goes straight to the top of the inbox.
7. That's it. Next, see how to mark a ticket solved.
