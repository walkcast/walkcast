# Tutorial plan: Acme Projects

Status: **approved** on 2026-10-29 · Audience: Team leads trying Acme Projects for the first time · Role for every item: owner

Four videos (about 2 minutes 40 seconds in total) and two SOPs, in learning order: getting started, everyday work, then admin.

**Left out:** Share, Search projects, the Reports date range and Change plan don't work yet. Signing in and logging out is a plain email and password form, and every tour starts signed in.

**Before recording:** restart the sample app, so projects, tasks and invites from earlier runs are gone.

## 1. Create your first project

`create-your-first-project` · Video · ~55 s

**Goal:** After this video you can create a project and add its first task.
**Audience and role:** New team leads, as owner
**Prerequisites:** Signed in
**Demo data:** Only the three starting projects, with no "Office move" project from an earlier run. Three members, so the Owner list has choices.

**Steps**
1. Start on the Dashboard
2. Click New project
3. Enter a Project name and a Description
4. Choose an Owner
5. Click Create project, and the new project opens
6. Under New task, type a first task and click Add task

**Opening:** Everything in Acme lives in a project. Let's create your first one and give it a task.
**Closing:** That's your first project, with its first task. Next, find a project and tick off tasks.

## 2. Find a project and tick off tasks

`find-a-project-and-tick-off-tasks` · Video · ~35 s

**Goal:** After this video you can find any project and mark its tasks done.
**Audience and role:** New team leads, as owner
**Prerequisites:** Signed in
**Demo data:** Website relaunch with its three tasks, none ticked yet.

**Steps**
1. Open Projects from the sidebar
2. Check each project's Owner and number of Tasks
3. Click Website relaunch to open it
4. Tick Write homepage copy to mark it done

**Opening:** Your projects are always a click away. Here's how to find one and tick off finished tasks.
**Closing:** You can also jump into a project from Recent projects on the Dashboard. Next, see how your team is doing.

## 3. Track progress and export a CSV

`track-progress-and-export` · Video · ~35 s

**Goal:** After this video you can check how your team is doing and download your projects as a CSV file.
**Audience and role:** New team leads, as owner
**Prerequisites:** Signed in
**Demo data:** The three starting projects with their tasks, so the CSV has rows.

**Steps**
1. Start on the Dashboard and read the headline numbers
2. Open Reports from the sidebar
3. Read Tasks completed and Average time to close
4. Click Export CSV to download every project with its task count

**Opening:** Want to know how your team is doing? Acme shows you in two places.
**Closing:** Open the file in any spreadsheet app to share it. Next, invite your team.

The CSV holds each project's task count, not the Reports figures, and the narration says so. The recording shows the click but not the downloaded file.

## 4. Invite a teammate

`invite-a-teammate` · Video and SOP · ~35 s

**Goal:** After this, you can invite someone by email and choose their role.
**Audience and role:** Workspace owners, as owner
**Prerequisites:** Signed in as the workspace owner
**Demo data:** An email address that isn't on the team yet, such as alex@example.com. No pending invites left over from an earlier run.

**Steps**
1. Open Settings from the sidebar
2. Click Members
3. Check who's already on the team and their roles
4. Under Invite a teammate, enter their Email address
5. Choose a Role, Member or Admin
6. Click Send invite
7. Check the confirmation and the new Invite pending row

**Opening:** Projects move faster with your team in them. Here's how to invite someone.
**Closing:** Invite the rest of your team the same way.

The app doesn't define what Admins can do that Members can't, so the narration names the two roles without explaining them.

## 5. Check your plan and invoices

`find-and-check-billing` · SOP

**Goal:** Confirm the current plan, seat count and renewal date, and look up a past invoice amount.
**Audience and role:** Workspace owners, as owner
**Prerequisites:** Signed in as the workspace owner
**Demo data:** At least two past invoices.

**Steps**
1. Open Settings from the sidebar
2. Click Billing
3. Read the plan, seat count and renewal date at the top
4. Find the month you need in the Invoice table and read its Amount

Invoices can only be read in the table; there's no download. Changing the plan isn't covered because Change plan doesn't work yet.
