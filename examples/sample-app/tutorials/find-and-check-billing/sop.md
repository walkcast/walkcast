# Check your plan and invoices

**Purpose:** Confirm the current plan, seat count and renewal date, and look up the amount of a past invoice.
**Scope:** When someone asks what Acme Projects costs, or you need a past invoice amount for your records.
**Roles:** Workspace owner.
**Prerequisites:** You're signed in.

## Procedure

1. In the sidebar, click **Settings**.
2. Click **Billing**.

   ![Billing on the Settings page](step:2)

3. Read the line at the top of the card. It shows the plan name, the number of seats and the renewal date.

   ![The plan, seats and renewal date](step:3)

4. In the table, find the month you need under **Invoice** and read its **Amount**.

   ![The invoice table](step:4)

## Expected result

You have the plan name, seat count and renewal date, plus the amount for the month you looked up.

## Troubleshooting

- **You need a copy of an invoice:** invoices can't be opened or downloaded from **Billing**. Note the month and **Amount** from the table instead.
- **You want to change the plan:** the **Change plan** button doesn't work yet.
