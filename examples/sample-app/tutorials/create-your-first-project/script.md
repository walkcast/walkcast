# Create your first project

Audience: New team leads · Length: about 55 seconds · Role: owner

1. Everything in Acme lives in a project. Let's create your first one and give it a task.
2. You start on the Dashboard, which sums up your open projects and tasks.
3. Click New project.
4. Give it a name your team will recognize.
5. Add a short description, so everyone knows what it covers.
6. Choose an owner. Their name appears next to the project in every list.
7. Click Create project, and your new project opens.
8. It has no tasks yet. Type your first one under New task.
9. Click Add task, and it's on the list.
10. That's your first project, with its first task. Next, find a project and tick off tasks.
