# Track progress and export a CSV

Audience: New team leads · Length: about 35 seconds · Role: owner

1. Want to know how your team is doing? Acme shows you in two places.
2. The Dashboard has the headline numbers, like open projects, tasks due this week, and team members.
3. For more detail, open Reports from the sidebar.
4. Here you see how many tasks were completed, and the average time to close one.
5. Click Export CSV to download every project with its number of tasks.
6. Open the file in any spreadsheet app to share it. Next, invite your team.
