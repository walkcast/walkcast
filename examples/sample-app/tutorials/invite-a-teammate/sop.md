# Invite a teammate

**Purpose:** Give someone access to Acme Projects with the right role.
**Scope:** Whenever someone joins a team that works in Acme Projects.
**Roles:** Workspace owner.
**Prerequisites:** You're signed in, and you have the person's work email address.

## Procedure

1. In the sidebar, click **Settings**.
2. Click **Members**.

   ![Members on the Settings page](step:3)

3. Check the table to make sure the person isn't already listed, either as a member or as **Invite pending**.

   ![The members table](step:4)

4. Under **Invite a teammate**, enter their address in **Email address**.

   ![The Email address field under Invite a teammate](step:5)

5. In **Role**, choose **Member** or **Admin**. The Members page has no way to change a role later, so choose carefully.
6. Click **Send invite**.

## Expected result

A message at the top reads **Invite sent to** followed by their email address. The members table shows a new row with **Invite pending**, their email address and the role you chose.

![The confirmation and the new Invite pending row](step:7:after)

Acme doesn't email the person, so let them know you've invited them.

## Troubleshooting

- **Send invite doesn't do anything:** **Email address** is required and must be a complete email address. Fix the address and click **Send invite** again.
- **The message says the person "is already on the team or invited":** they're already in the members table, so no new invite was added. Find their row to check their role.
