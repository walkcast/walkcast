# Invite a teammate

Audience: Workspace owners · Length: about 35 seconds · Role: owner

1. Projects move faster with your team in them. Here's how to invite someone.
2. Open Settings from the sidebar.
3. Then click Members.
4. Here's everyone on the team, with their email and role.
5. To add someone, enter their email address under Invite a teammate.
6. Choose their role, Member or Admin.
7. Click Send invite.
8. Acme confirms the invite and lists them as Invite pending, with the role you chose.
9. Invite the rest of your team the same way.
