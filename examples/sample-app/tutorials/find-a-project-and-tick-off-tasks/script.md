# Find a project and tick off tasks

Audience: New team leads · Length: about 35 seconds · Role: owner

1. Your projects are always a click away. Here's how to find one and tick off finished tasks.
2. Open Projects from the sidebar.
3. Every project is listed with its owner and how many tasks it has.
4. Click a project's name to open it.
5. When a task is done, tick its box. It's crossed off and saved right away.
6. You can also jump into a project from Recent projects on the Dashboard. Next, see how your team is doing.
